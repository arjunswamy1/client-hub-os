# SpektraFin Final Expense — Fact-Checked Campaign Dataset
Prepared 2026-09-16. All figures pulled from stored records only. No estimates, no fills.

**Scope note:** the Final Expense campaign in this account is **SpektraFin Final Expense**, bought on **Roku CTV** and tracked in **RingFuel**. It is not a BuyologyIQ campaign; BuyologyIQ's records (Walk-In Tubs / Bathroom Remodel, Meta + Ringba) are a separate client and are excluded. If the case study is meant to be published under BuyologyIQ's name for this client, the underlying data is the SpektraFin set below.

**Platforms with stored data:** Roku only (`roku_daily_metrics`, 27 campaigns).
**Universal Ads:** no rows exist for this client in any Universal Ads table — **unavailable, not zero.**
**Timezones:** calls counted in America/Puerto_Rico (RingFuel's reporting clock). Roku bills in America/Los_Angeles. A single day's spend and calls are therefore up to ~4 hours out of step; weekly and monthly figures absorb this, daily figures do not.

## Definitions applied

| Term | Rule used | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Media spend | Roku billed spend, per day | Complete Aug 5 – Sep 16 |
| Revenue | RingFuel's own reported payout per call ($16 or $23, set by buyer accept) | Complete Sep 2 – Sep 14 only |
| Paid / billable call | RingFuel payout > $0 | Same window |
| Duration bands | Calls ≥60s, ≥90s, ≥120s reported separately | Complete Sep 2 – Sep 16 |
| Gross profit | revenue − media spend | |
| ROAS | revenue ÷ media spend | |

**The billable-duration threshold is UNKNOWN for this client.** There is no economics record for SpektraFin, and the data disproves a duration rule: 1,242 calls with $0 payout include calls up to 4,381 seconds, while paid calls start at 1 second. Payment is driven by buyer accept, not talk time. Consequently:
- Billable-call rate is reported as **paid-call rate** (platform-reported payout), plus 60s/90s/120s duration bands as a separate sensitivity, never merged.
- No contractually-confirmed billable definition exists, so no verdict (scale / hold / cut) is issued in this document.

**Payout rule change:** the operator states the current rule is **$16 per raw call**. The historical export prices calls at **$16 or $23 per accepted call** ($66,696 = 1,788 × $16 + 1,656 × $23 — reconciles exactly). The effective date of the $16/raw-call rule is **not documented**, so it is not applied retroactively; where useful it is shown as a clearly-labelled alternate basis.

# SECTION 1 — EXECUTIVE FINDINGS

1. **True launch date: 2026-08-10.** First Roku billed spend ($507.55) on the legacy Final Expense campaigns ("08.10 - FE - Legacy 7 / Legacy 8", own tracking numbers 17813176712 / 13309440037). Campaigns were created Aug 5; spend began Aug 10.
2. **August outcome is unmeasurable.** August spend is $4,064.63 across Aug 10–22. **Zero call records exist for August** — no RingFuel rows, and no calls on the August tracking numbers anywhere in the system. August is a total loss of record ($4,064.63 spend, revenue unknown), not a measured loss.
3. **Measured campaign begins 2026-09-02**, with the restructured Roku buy on a single tracking number (+1 888 662 0024). First calls: Sep 2 (5 calls, no spend recorded that day).
4. **First profitable day: 2026-09-03** — spend $2,111.36, revenue $4,646, profit **+$2,534.64**, ROAS 2.20. This is the first day on which both spend and revenue exist.
5. **First profitable week: 2026-08-31 → 2026-09-06** — spend $11,715.01, revenue $17,871, profit **+$6,155.99**, ROAS 1.53.
6. **First profitable calendar month: none yet.** September to date shows spend $77,092.46 against recorded revenue $66,696 (−$10,396.46) — but that deficit is an artifact of missing revenue data for Sep 14–16 (see item 9), not a measured loss. August: loss of $4,064.63 with no revenue data.
7. **Four consecutive profitable weeks: not achieved, and not yet testable.** Only **two complete weeks** of paired spend-and-revenue data exist (Aug 31–Sep 6 and Sep 7–Sep 13). Both were profitable. The third week (Sep 14 onward) has no revenue data. The honest statement is: **the campaign was profitable in every week for which revenue is available, but the four-week sustained test cannot be evaluated until revenue capture is repaired.**
8. **Time from launch to first profitability: 24 days** (Aug 10 → Sep 3). **Payback date: 2026-09-04** — cumulative profit crossed zero at **+$1,644.15**, recovering the full $4,064.63 of August spend on the second profitable day. So yes: later profit did more than offset the early testing spend, within two days of measured trading.
9. **Cumulative position through the last day with complete data (2026-09-13): +$7,283.72** on $52,420.28 spend and $59,704 revenue — **ROAS 1.14**, margin 12.2% of revenue, $1.79 margin per raw call. From Sep 14 the running total falls to −$14,461.10, driven entirely by $28,736.82 of spend booked against $6,992 of revenue in a window where revenue is known to be missing. **Do not report −$14,461 as a result.**
10. **Best period after reaching scale: 2026-09-08** — spend $7,027.55, 684 calls, 478 paid, revenue $10,994, profit **+$3,966.45**, ROAS 1.56, cost per paid call $14.70. Second best: Sep 7 (+$3,287.77, ROAS 1.59).

# DATA-QUALITY FLAGS

1. **August call data missing entirely.** $4,064.63 of spend with no calls of record. The August tracking numbers (17813176712, 13309440037, 1424955387) return zero rows in every call table. Launch-phase efficiency cannot be stated.
2. **Revenue stops on 2026-09-14.** The live RingFuel webhook is configured with a custom body sending only `call_id`, `payout`, `duration`, `state`. 1,367 calls (Sep 14: 234, Sep 15: 1,055, Sep 16: 78) carry $0 revenue, blank dialed number, blank campaign, blank buyer, and no connected/converted flag. Sep 14 revenue is understated because 234 of its 857 calls came through this path. **Fix: clear the custom body in RingFuel so the standard payload posts.** Until then, no result should be quoted past Sep 13.
3. **No economics record for SpektraFin.** Payout terms, revenue source and billable definition are unrecorded. Blocked outputs: billable-duration threshold, margin-per-call verdicts, allowable cost per call, scale/cut recommendations.
4. **Two payout rules in play.** Historical: $16 or $23 per accepted call. Stated current: $16 per raw call. Effective date of the change is undocumented. On the $16/raw-call basis, Sep 3–13 would be $65,008 revenue (+$12,587.72, ROAS 1.24) rather than $59,704 — a materially different read. Confirm the basis with RingFuel before publishing either.
5. **Duplicate / repeat callers:** 4,686 historical calls come from 3,544 distinct caller numbers — **1,142 repeat calls (24.4%)**. Repeat callers are counted as separate calls, as RingFuel counts them.
6. **Non-productive call states:** 477 `no-answer`, 6 `in-progress`, 1 `failed`, 1 `early-media`. No `canceled` or `no-route` dispositions are recorded.
7. **Connected/converted flags disagree with RingFuel's UI.** For Sep 8–14 our records show 2,281 converted / 2,730 connected against RingFuel's 2,339 / 3,237 — their connected count includes early-media calls with no talk time. Paid-call counts, which drive revenue, do reconcile.
8. **Reconciliation performed:** RingFuel export revenue $66,696 = 1,788 × $16 + 1,656 × $23 — exact. Roku spend totals $81,157.10 against 27 campaigns, re-pulled from Roku Aug 5 – Sep 16. Roku's zero-spend days (Aug 15–17, Aug 23–31, Sep 1–2, Sep 6, Sep 12–13, Sep 16) were verified against Roku's own response, which returns zero billed spend and zero impressions — genuine dark days, not sync gaps.
9. **Sep 7 anomaly:** $5,590.23 spend with 509 calls is internally consistent; no gap.
10. **Creative-level analysis impossible.** RingFuel calls carry no creative or ad identifier — only a dialed number and one campaign name. Only two creatives are named anywhere (U17v2, Vid089v2, both August). No creative-level performance claim can be made.
11. **No Universal Ads or Meta data** for this campaign. Roku is the only spend source of record.
